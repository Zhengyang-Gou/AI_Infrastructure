# 07 · Hands-on Projects

## Learning Objectives

- Turn theory, source reading, and profiling into reproducible, measurable projects.
- Produce engineering evidence suitable for resumes and interviews.

## Prerequisites

- Phases 1–5; distributed projects also require Phase 6.

## Recommended Learning Order

1. vLLM Performance Benchmark.
2. vLLM Source Modification.
3. Mini LLM Serving.

## Key Questions

- Are the baseline, controlled variables, workload, and statistics reliable?
- Where does the gain come from, and does it reproduce across models and workloads?

## Interview Focus

- Present context, target, bottleneck, solution, difficulty, result, and reflection.
- Explain failed experiments and trade-offs, not only the best result.
