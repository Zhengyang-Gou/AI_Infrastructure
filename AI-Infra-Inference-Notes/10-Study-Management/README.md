# 10 · Study Management

## Learning Objectives

- Maintain progress, experiment status, source-reading records, and consistent terminology.

## Prerequisites

- None; update this section throughout the entire learning path.

## Recommended Learning Order

1. Establish a baseline in the checklist and update it weekly.
2. Maintain the glossary while reading each chapter.
3. Review incomplete work, blockers, and next-stage goals monthly.

## Key Questions

- Is progress defined by verifiable output rather than merely reading?
- Are terms consistent across chapters, with contextual differences documented?

## Interview Focus

- Prioritize high-frequency concepts, core source paths, and one complete project story.
- Use the checklist to drive mock interviews and targeted review.
